Operational Boundaries & Anti-Bias Directives

<operational_boundaries_and_anti_bias> — Non-negotiable econometric rules
Mandatory Rules (MR-01 to MR-04)
MR-01 Confidence Protocol
Strict Dual-Verification Protocol (DVP)

No numerical claim regarding candidate pool size, average CTC, notice period duration, or college intake can be asserted based on a single speculative data point. Every estimate must be cross-verified or explicitly framed with an evidentiary confidence interval (e.g., "Confidence: High [verified across Naukri hiring index + AmbitionBox salary telemetry]").

MR-02 Anti-Boosterism
Zero-Tolerance for City Boosterism

Reject generic superlatives such as "Kochi is rapidly emerging", "Trichy has cheap abundant engineers", "Coimbatore has a world-class talent pool", or "Bengaluru has unlimited supply". Quantify every market condition: provide explicit headcount estimates, active-to-passive ratios, compensation brackets (INR LPA), and attrition/retention rates.

MR-03 Behavioral Modeling
Grounded Relocation Behavioral Modeling

Do not assume candidates will relocate simply because a job exists. Relocation feasibility must be modeled through geo-economic realities: commute corridors (Kerala-CBE connectivity via NH 544 & Southern Railway Palakkad division; Central/West TN corridors such as Salem-CBE via NH 544, Trichy-CBE via NH 81 / Karur rail link, and Madurai-CBE via NH 83), cost-of-living differentials, cultural/linguistic overlap, family stability factors, and historical offer-to-join (OTJ) ratios.

MR-04 Evidentiary Proof
Proof-by-Quote & Sourcing Evidence

For market assertions, cite specific industry entities, technological parks (Infopark Phase 1/2, Technopark, TIDEL Park CBE, Siruseri SIPCOT, ELCOT IT Parks in Navalpattu-Trichy, Jagirammapalayam-Salem, and Vadapalanji-Madurai), regional wage surveys, official college engineering seat allocations (TNEA / KEAM), and named employer ecosystems.

5-Phase Research Execution Workflow

<research_execution_workflow> — End-to-end analytical tasks & feeder market benchmarking
01

Requirement Decomposition & Skill Taxonomy Engineering

PHASE-1 • 2 Core Decomposition Tasks
T1.1
Seniority Stratification: Parse the 376 requisitions and group them into normalized seniority tiers: Junior (1-3 yrs), Mid-Senior (4-7 yrs), Lead/Principal (8-11 yrs), and Enterprise Architect/Executive (12+ yrs).
T1.2
Prerequisite Friction Mapping: Map specific prerequisite combinations that heighten hiring friction (e.g., .NET + Azure AI + Event-Driven Architecture or BFSI Solution Architect + CXO Presales).
02

Coimbatore Local Talent Landscape & Deficit Audit

PHASE-2 • Local Strengths, Structural Deficits & Scarcity Index
T2.1
Audit CBE's Local Supply Strengths: Detail roles where CBE has deep, self-sustaining talent pools (e.g., Core Java/.NET, Manual & Selenium QA, Embedded systems, IT Service Desk, BPO/Operations) driven by local campuses and established employers (Cognizant, Bosch, Wipro, Cameron, Pricol, KGISL).
T2.2
Audit CBE's Structural Deficits: Identify where local supply breaks down (e.g., Microsoft Fabric/Azure AI squads, ServiceNow specialized implementation engineers, presales Solution Architects with Tier-1 consulting background).
T2.3
Talent Scarcity Index: Quantify the Talent Scarcity Index (Scale 1-10) for each requirement cluster in Coimbatore, factoring in Time-to-Fill (TTF) and local market depth.
03

Comparative Feeder Market Benchmarking (Kerala, Metros & Tier-2 TN Feeder Hubs)

PHASE-3 • 3 Feeder Tiers Evaluated Across 4 Structural Dimensions

For every identified skill deficit in Coimbatore, evaluate candidate feeder locations across three distinct tiers:
1. Kerala Feeder Corridors: Kochi (Infopark/SmartCity) & Trivandrum (Technopark).
2. Tier-1 Metros: Chennai (OMR/Guindy/Ambattur) & Bengaluru (Whitefield/ECity/ORR).
3. Emerging Tier-2 Tamil Nadu Feeder Hubs: Top 2–3 high-gravity intra-state feeder markets—specifically Trichy (Navalpattu ELCOT / NIT corridor), Salem (Jagirammapalayam ELCOT / Sona / NH 544 West TN corridor), and Madurai (Vadapalanji ELCOT / HCLTech & Honeywell corridor)—or dynamically determine the optimal secondary TN feeder city based on skill depth, campus concentration, and lower cost-arbitrage / high relocation willingness to CBE.

💰 DIM-1: Cost & Compensation
  • Median Base CTC by YOE (3-5, 6-9, 10-14 yrs) across Metros vs. Kochi vs. Tier-2 TN (Trichy, Salem, Madurai)
  • Average Expected Hike % for lateral move to Coimbatore
  • Total Cost to Hire (relocation allowance + buyouts)
  • Cost variance % relative to Coimbatore benchmarks
⚡ DIM-2: Skill Depth & Complexity
  • Tier-1 vs. Tier-2/3 consulting presence across markets
  • Global enterprise prod vs. maintenance/legacy support
  • Code-level engineering maturity (Microservices, Cloud, AI/ML)
🌊 DIM-3: Bandwidth & Pool Depth
  • Estimated addressable headcount in target cities (Kochi, TVM, MAA, BLR, Trichy, Salem, MDU)
  • Active job-seeker liquidity vs. passive talent
  • Competing employer density (counter-offer risk)
🚆 DIM-4: Behavioral & Relocation
  • Relocation Propensity Score to CBE (L/M/H) by market
  • Commute/Road travel time (NH 544, NH 81, NH 83 & Rail)
  • Notice Period Compliance & Ghosting index
  • Realistic OTJ % (On-site vs. Hybrid models)
04

Institutional & Lateral Feeder Channel Mapping

PHASE-4 • Employer Ecosystems & Academic Pipelines
T4.1
Target Companies (Poaching & Lateral Sourcing List): Identify specific IT service firms, product engineering centers, and GCCs in Kochi, Trivandrum, Chennai, and emerging Tier-2 TN centers (e.g., Trichy: Vuram/WNS, Scientific Publishing; Madurai: HCLTech, Honeywell, Chella Software; Salem: Vee Technologies, Sona ecosystem) where candidates with identical stacks are clustered. Specify which companies operate with 60-90 day notice periods and where buyout openness exists.
T4.2
Target Academic Institutions (Early Career & 1-3 YOE Juniors): Map premier and Tier-2 engineering colleges across Coimbatore/Western TN (PSG Tech, CIT, Kumaraguru, Amrita, Sri Krishna), Central/Southern TN (NIT Trichy, Saranathan, SASTRA, TCE Madurai, Sona College Salem, GCE Salem), and the Kerala Feeder Corridor (Model Engineering College Thrikkakara, SCMS, Rajagiri, GEC Thrissur, NSS Palakkad, TKM Kollam). Provide estimated circuit/CS intake capacities, curriculum alignment with modern cloud/AI, and historical campus placement conversion data.
05

Recruitment Analyst Operational Playbook

PHASE-5 • Actionable Recruiter Playbook, Search Strings & Scripts
T5.1
Sourcing Decision Tree: Construct a decision framework guiding recruiters: When to hire locally in CBE, when to tap intra-state Tier-2 TN feeders (Trichy, Salem, Madurai) for cost arbitrage and high retention, when to tap the Kerala corridor (Kochi/Trivandrum), and when to escalate to Tier-1 Metros (Bengaluru/Chennai/Remote).
T5.2
Boolean & Semantic Search Strings: Provide actionable search strings for LinkedIn Recruiter / Naukri / Foundit for the top 3 hardest-to-fill profiles, incorporating regional geographical radius filters for TN & Kerala feeder corridors.
T5.3
Relocation Pitch Scripts & Value Proposition: Formulate concrete pitch scripts and value propositions tailored for both Kerala candidates and Tier-2 TN candidates (Trichy/Salem/Madurai) relocating to Coimbatore (focusing on cost-of-living parity, proximity, quality of life, schooling, highway/rail connectivity, and traffic differentials).
